How Tagtruth works. We read each price in a deals post that names the store (almost all from 9to5Toys, some cross-checked with TechBargains), note when the post went up and when we read it, and link to the product's own page at that store, exactly as the source linked it. A deal whose source linked only to a sale landing page is left out. The verdict comes from the price history the source reports (a new low, near its best, or a fair cut), and a separate flag marks a “was” price the source says overstates the saving. Prices change fast on Black Friday; the store's own page is the final word.

Tagtruth is independent and has no affiliate links.
